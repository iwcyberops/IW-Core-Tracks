## 📌 What is This Book?
Written by master strategist **Robert Greene**, *The Laws of Human Nature* is an exhaustive codebook of human behavior. 

Most people walk through life sleepwalking. We believe we make rational decisions, that our friends always want the best for us, and that our enemies are obvious. **This book shatters that illusion.**

It proves that under our polite modern clothes and technology, humans are still driven by the same primitive forces that governed us hundreds of thousands of years ago: **fear, envy, vanity, lust for power, tribal aggression, and self-delusion**.

## 🧭 The 4 Universal Truths (Overarching Meta-Rules)

These 4 core insights run through every single law in this book. If you remember nothing else, remember these:

```
[ 1. WE ARE DUAL BEINGS ]      ---> Lower Animal Self (reactive) vs. Higher Self (rational).
[ 2. EMOTIONS RULE FIRST ]     ---> We decide with emotion, then invent logic to defend it.
[ 3. PEOPLE NEVER CHANGE ]     ---> Character is repetitive; what someone did once, they will repeat.
[ 4. PEOPLE LIVE IN THEATER ]  ---> Everyone wears a mask; never judge anyone by their words alone.
```

---

### 1. The Reality of the Dual Self
Inside every human are two opposing forces:
* **The Lower Self:** Weak, reactive, easily offended, hungry for instant pleasure, and easily infected by crowd panic.
* **The Higher Self:** Thoughtful, calm, observant, creative, and focused on long-term goals.
* **The Goal:** Cultivate your **Inner Authority** so the Higher Self guides the Lower Self (The Rider guiding the Horse).

### 2. The 3 Universal Self-Opinions (The Iron Rule of Persuasion)
Every person on Earth holds three deep beliefs about themselves:
1. *"I act of my own free will (I am autonomous)."*
2. *"I am intelligent and have common sense."*
3. *"I am basically a good, moral, and decent person."*
* **The Golden Law:** **Never challenge these three beliefs directly.** If you make someone feel stupid, forced, or evil, they will become your permanent enemy. Always confirm their self-opinion to lower resistance.

### 3. Character is Destiny (The Repetition Rule)
* People will make promises, cry, apologize, and swear they have changed.
* **Do not believe words.** Look at their last 5 years of actions under stress. If someone micromanaged, panicked, backstabbed, or quit in the past, they are neurologically wired to repeat that exact pattern.

### 4. Treat People as Facts of Nature
* If it rains or snows, you do not take it personally or scream at the sky; you simply open an umbrella.
* Treat toxic narcissists, bullies, critics, and slackers the exact same way: **they are natural phenomena**. Study their behavior like a scientist and outmaneuver them calmly.
